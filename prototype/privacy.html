Privacy policy

Draft for the pre-launch test. Last updated 2026-10-19.

This is a plain-language draft, not legal advice. It should be reviewed by a BC lawyer before RideShare is opened to the public.

What RideShare is

RideShare is a non-profit commuter carpool pilot in Metro Vancouver. Drivers share the cost of a trip they are already making. Nobody makes a profit.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and first name (from Google sign-in or an email link). We never see your Google password.
  • Your profile: a photo (one you upload, or your Google photo), and if you drive, your car's make, colour and licence plate. These are self-reported and are not verified by us.
  • Trips and ride requests: the places, times and dates, your detour limits, and the pickup and drop-off points. A point you pick with the address search or "Use my location" is stored with its map coordinates, not only a neighbourhood name.
  • Rides and points: who rode with whom, the date, the status, any meeting spot and note a rider adds, and your points balance and history.
  • Messages between the driver and rider of a ride, ratings (good or not good) and reports you send about a ride.
  • Live location (while you drive): your phone's position while a ride is in progress. The latest position is shown to the riders on that ride. We also keep a short summary of each drive (start and end time, distance and the last position).
  • "Show my location" and the GPS test page: your position stays on your own device and is not sent to us.

What we do with it

We use it only to run the service: matching drivers and riders, showing a ride's progress, moving points, and keeping the service safe (for example limiting spam). We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Who can see what

  • Any signed-in person can see your first name, and the places (to the nearest few hundred metres) and times of trips or requests you have switched on.
  • The person you are matched with (once a seat is accepted, and for 30 days after the ride) can see your photo, first name and, if you drive, your car. They can also see your ride details, any meeting spot and note, and your messages. The riders on a ride see the driver's live location during the ride.
  • Ratings: other people see only a percentage and a count, not who gave it.
  • Reports are read by the RideShare administrator. They are not shown to the person you report.
  • Only you can see your email, points balance and history.

Where it is stored, and who helps us

Your data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) in the United States (West). The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Maps and routes come from Google Maps Platform, which receives the map requests your device makes. Sign-in with Google is handled by Google.

How long we keep it

Account data, ride history, messages, ratings and reports are kept until you delete your account, except that we may keep reports and the records needed to settle a dispute for as long as the matter needs. The driver's latest live position is deleted when a ride ends; the short drive summary is kept with the ride record. Expired "leave now" trips are hidden immediately. We do not yet delete old messages automatically.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and data: in the app, open the Points tab and use "Delete my account" (you type DELETE to confirm). This erases your profile, points, history, trips, requests and rides. It is not possible while you have an accepted ride that is not finished.
  • Location: you can block location in your phone or browser settings at any time. Riders will then not see your position.

Safety

Data is sent over HTTPS. Access to your information is restricted by database rules so that people only see what is listed above. If you find a security problem, please report it as described in the project's SECURITY.md on GitHub.
