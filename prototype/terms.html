Terms of use

Draft for the pre-launch test. Last updated 2026-10-19.

This is a plain-language draft, not legal advice. Cost-sharing rules, insurance and passenger liability in BC must be reviewed by a lawyer before RideShare is opened to the public.

1. A pilot, not a taxi service

RideShare is a non-profit pilot that helps people who already drive to work share the cost of the trip. Drivers do not make a profit and RideShare is not a taxi, ride-hailing or transportation company. Drivers are not our employees.

2. Who can use it

  • You must be an adult, and drivers must hold a valid driver's licence and have valid insurance for the vehicle.
  • You are responsible for your own insurance. Check with your insurer that carpooling for cost-sharing is covered.

3. Costs and points

  • Riders only pay their fair share of the real cost of the trip, never more. Cash is settled directly between the people in the car, outside the app.
  • Points are a closed-loop way to share costs. 1 point equals $0.10 of trip cost. Points cannot be cashed out, sold or transferred, and have no cash value.
  • Points are held when a driver accepts a ride and paid to the driver when the ride is completed.

4. Cancellations

  • Cancel more than 2 hours before pickup: all points returned.
  • Cancel within 2 hours: your first one each calendar month is free; after that the driver keeps half of the points.
  • You cannot cancel after the pickup time. A driver may mark a no-show after waiting.
  • If a driver cancels, or is very late and has not arrived, the rider gets all points back. Repeated cancellations show as a warning next to a name.

5. Your conduct

Be respectful and honest. Do not post false trips, spam, harass others, or misuse the service. We may remove accounts that do.

6. Safety and responsibility

Use your own judgment about who you ride with. RideShare does not check drivers or riders and cannot guarantee anyone's behaviour, the condition of a vehicle, or that a ride will happen. Share your trip details with someone you trust if you wish. In an emergency, call 911. To the extent allowed by law, RideShare is provided as-is during this pilot.

7. Your information

See the privacy policy. You can delete your account and data at any time from the Points tab.

8. Changes

This is a pilot, so features, points and these terms may change. We will update this page when they do.
