# RideShare driver UX review

Source: code in prototype/live.js and index.html (driverNow, driverForm, driverTop, planData, renderRequests, reqFit, leaveLine, resetTrip, startNav, wire). The mock at localhost:8791 turned out to be an old static prototype (Riders along your route / Accept list, no trip forms), so it was not useful for the live flows. Findings below come from reading the code, not from tapping the live app. Items marked (verify) need a check on a phone.

## Top 5

1. Request card has no date, no ready window and no rider-pickup time (Home, renderRequests line ~669)
   - Wrong: the driver sees name, "pickup to dropoff", points, detour. For a fixed-time trip the card never says WHICH DAY the rider wants (a rider can ask for next Tuesday while you are looking at today) or when the rider is ready. "They are ready until X" only appears inside the leave line, and only for some cases.
   - Fix: add one line at the top of each request: "Tue Nov 10, ready 7:30 to 8:00 am". Then one line "You pick them up about 7:48, drop off 8:20". Two lines, nothing else.
   - Effort: small.

2. Accept is a one-tap with no consequence shown, and cannot be undone cleanly (Home + Riders list)
   - Wrong: tap Accept, toast says "Accepted". On a flexible trip this also fixes your leave time for good, but the card only says that in small text above the button. There is no "what happens now" and no way to see the new plan without scrolling. Decline is also one tap with no reason option.
   - Fix: make the accept line the headline of the card ("Accept = you leave at 7:48 Tue. Earn 17 pts."), and after accepting show a short confirmation card: "Done. Plan updated. You leave Tue 7:48." with a "See plan" button. Keep Decline one tap.
   - Effort: small to medium.

3. Seat requests are shown in three places, and the copy in the Riders tab is much poorer (driverLists line ~1004)
   - Wrong: Home top card shows pickup and dropoff, tags, detour and leave line. The "Seat requests" card in the Riders list shows only name, pay and detour: no places, no tags. A driver who opens the second one cannot decide. Two versions of the same thing also drift apart.
   - Fix: one shared function draws the request card and both places use it (or remove the Riders-tab one and link to Home).
   - Effort: small.

4. Stale/early cards clutter the top of Home (renderRequests "waiting", driverTop, plan)
   - Wrong: "Time to leave" appears as the card title for any accepted rider on any future date (it reads accepted rides with ride_date >= today). On a trip three days away the top of Home says "Time to leave" while the real instruction is "nothing to do yet". On a trip day you also get three stacked things: the request/leave banner, "Your trip" card, then "Trip plan" card, each with Start trip and Reset trip. Today there are two Start trip buttons (driverTop and plan) and two Reset trip buttons on the same screen, plus an "Open Google Maps again" ghost button.
   - Fix: one "Next trip" card on Home, that changes by state: waiting for riders, plan ready (future), leave soon (within about 2 hours: only then show Start trip and the leave banner), under way. Only the card for the current state is shown. The leave banner should only appear when leave time is within roughly 2 hours. One Start trip button, one overflow "Cancel trip" link.
   - Effort: medium.

5. "Reset trip" is the only way out and it does too much (driverTop, planData, resetTrip)
   - Wrong: Reset trip is used for cancel, for "something went wrong", and for finishing. It silently cancels every accepted rider, and the wording ("Reset") does not tell a normal person that real people get a cancellation. One confirm dialog (browser window.confirm) with a wall of text. Separately, "Edit trip" then "Cancel" in the edit form means "close the form", not cancel the trip. Two different meanings of Cancel.
   - Fix: rename to "Cancel this trip". Dialog says plainly: "2 riders (Alex, Sam) will be told. They get their points back. Cancelling often shows a warning next to your name." Rename the edit form's button to "Close" or "Discard changes". Keep a separate small "End trip" for after the drive.
   - Effort: small.

## Remaining items (6 to 15)

6. Detour cost shown, but not what it means for the driver's own arrival (reqFit / detourLine)
   - Wrong: "+5 min detour, total +9 (limit 15)". Good, but the driver's first rule is "my arrival comes first". The card never says "You still arrive by 8:12 (your target 8:30)".
   - Fix: add "You arrive 8:12" (and, when the driver used Arrive by, "your deadline 8:30, 18 min spare"). Colour only when tight.
   - Effort: small.

7. Edit trip loses "Arrive by" (driverForm line ~970 passes mode 'leave' always)
   - Wrong: a trip posted as "Arrive by 9:00" is stored as a leave time. Edit it later and the form shows "Leave at 7:42", and the arrival goal is gone. Re-saving silently turns it into a leave-time trip.
   - Fix: store the mode (leave/arrive) and the arrive time on the trip, and show it in the trip summary ("Arrive by 9:00 (leave about 7:42)").
   - Effort: medium (needs a stored field).

8. Editing a trip that already has accepted riders has no warning (lt-confirm, line ~1262)
   - Wrong: Confirm and update just writes the new row. Change the date or time and accepted riders are affected, with no message about it.
   - Fix: if riders are accepted, block changes to date/time/places or show "3 riders accepted. Changing this cancels them" and require a confirm. Allow changing only limits and payment without a warning.
   - Effort: medium.

9. Flexible window: the confirm screen and Home do not explain what happens next (tripWhen, flexBox)
   - Wrong: "Any time between 8:00 am and 2:00 pm. Set by your first rider" is understandable but there is no way to see, before anyone asks, whether anyone could fit. After the first rider is accepted the trip is locked and the window cannot be re-opened (flex_locked hides the control entirely, so the driver has no explanation). Labels switch between "Earliest I can leave / Latest I can leave / I must arrive by" which is hard to follow for Arrive by plus window.
   - Fix: on the locked trip show one line: "Leave time fixed at 9:40 by Alex." On the form, write a one-line preview when the window is set ("Riders ready between 8:00 and 2:00 can be matched"). For arrive-by plus window, use a single sentence form: "Arrive between ___ and ___".
   - Effort: medium.

10. Pickup place wording and the privacy note on the plan (planData placeTxt, paxBlock)
   - Wrong: places now show label plus area, which is better, but the long privacy paragraph ("within about 100 m, never a house number") repeats under every plan. Pickup is also shown twice (stop row and passenger block). The meeting spot is a ghost button in the passenger block, easy to miss, and nothing tells the driver that the exact place is still undecided.
   - Fix: put "Meeting spot: not agreed yet, tap to set" directly under each pickup row. Remove the repeated privacy paragraph (keep it once, on the first accept or in Help). Do not repeat the passenger block if there is one passenger and the stop row already has name and place.
   - Effort: small.

11. "Preview route (watch the car)" and "Cost if driving alone / Your share now" in the Riders tab summary (driverLists line ~1000)
   - Wrong: the preview buttons are not gated by test mode, so every driver sees simulator controls. "Your share now" frames it as the driver paying a share, which can confuse a non-profit story.
   - Fix: hide the preview for real drivers; replace the stat block by "Total distance, total time, points you will earn".
   - Effort: small.

12. Day-of: Start trip hands over to Google Maps with no explanation of how to come back (startNav)
   - Wrong: the plan says "tap X in Maps first" (jargon) and "same button appears in your phone notification". A normal driver will not know how to return and tap Picked up. After Maps opens, nothing in-app tells them what is next. The Picked up and Dropped off buttons are big, which is good, but "Open Google Maps again" is a second small button that competes with them.
   - Fix: after Start trip, replace the plan with a single "Next stop" card: "Next: pick up Alex at X, 7:48" with buttons Open Maps / Picked up. Move the "tap X in Maps" tip to a one-time tooltip.
   - Effort: medium.

13. Leave-now (driverNow) vs Post a trip vs "Offer a ride now" vs "Plan ahead" (driverNow/driverForm)
   - Wrong: four different entry cards with similar names ("Where are you driving?", "Not on a schedule? Offer a ride now", "Plan ahead", "Post a trip"), two toggles ("Plan a trip for later instead" / "Leave now instead") and, after posting, a second form with a different shape ("More options" collapsed in one, expanded in the other). A driver with a posted trip is told to "use Start trip instead" of "Offer a ride now".
   - Fix: one card, one choice at the top: "Leaving now / Later", the same "More options" in both, and the same wording of the same screen.
   - Effort: medium.

14. Time formats and relative times beyond the leave line (fmtLeft, whenText)
   - Wrong: fmtLeft is now OK for "2 days 6 h", but "2 days" with no time of day is not what a driver wants to see in a banner. Elsewhere the time is "7:30 am" but the plan uses "Tomorrow 8:41 am". Different styles in different places.
   - Fix: for anything more than 6 hours away show the day and clock time only ("Tue 8:41 am"), and keep "in 35 min" for the last hour.
   - Effort: small.

15. Rider cancels, or the first rider drops out of a flexible trip (no copy found)
   - Wrong: I could not find any driver-facing message or screen for "your first rider cancelled; your leave time is open again / still fixed". The earlier-requests list only keeps them for 24 hours, and only inside a collapsed section.
   - Fix: a notification and a short Home line: "Alex cancelled. You are back to 'any time 8 to 2'" or "Your leave time stays 9:40 because Sam is still on". Show cancellations on Home until read.
   - Effort: medium (depends on whether the database resets the lock; verify).

## Missing things a driver would reasonably expect

- Share the plan with the rider: the rider gets a status, but the driver cannot send "I leave at 7:42, pick-up at X". Add a Share plan / message button on the passenger block.
- Total points the driver will earn: only shown after the trips (Points earned in history, line ~256). Show "You will earn 17 pts (if both ride)" on every request and on the plan.
- Edit the window after posting: not possible once the first rider locks it. At least say why and offer "Cancel and repost".
- A visible rider ready window on the plan stops (earliest, latest) and what happens if the rider is late (the driver may drive on, as the rider is told) - the driver is never told they may leave.
- Seats left: "1 of 3 seats taken" on the trip card.
- Re-offer after cancellations: a "Repost this trip" shortcut from the past trips list.
- Undo for Accept within about 1 minute without a cancellation mark.
- Decline reason (too far, time does not suit): optional, helps riders choose another driver.
- A single place to see the next 7 days of a repeating trip, with the riders for each day (planData shows only the next accepted day).

## Not tested

Nothing on the live app was tapped; the local mock is a different, older build. Items 8, 12 and 15 depend on server behaviour and should be checked on the phone.
