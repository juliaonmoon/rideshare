## What the reviews say we should do

1. **Recurring trips are a must.** Liftango users ask for it; manual daily entry is a top complaint. Our "set once, runs every weekday" idea targets a real gap.
2. **Cancellations kill trust** (Poparide). Needs clear no-show/cancel rules, notice windows and visible reliability scores.
3. **Empty app = dead app.** "Difficulty finding matches" shows up on every small carpool app. Launch employer-by-employer, and show riders *when* matches are likely.
4. **Simple setup and reliability** matter as much as features ("decent when it works").
5. **Trust wins ratings:** Poparide's 4.8★ comes from verified profiles, responsive support and accountability. Copy that.
