# Staged Plan — Spend Almost Nothing Until It's Proven

_2026-10-08. Goal: keep upfront investment minimal. Each stage has a **stop-or-go check** before any more money or time is spent. Costs are rough USD estimates._

| Stage | What happens | Cash out | Stop-or-go check |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Learn** (1–2 weeks) | Try the Liftango (TransLink) app with an EA email; test Loop; ask colleagues who drives in from Surrey; read your employment agreement on side projects / IP | **$0** | Are there enough colleagues with matching commutes? Is a side project allowed? |
| **1. Prototype** (2–6 weeks) | Build a simple version on free tiers (no store accounts yet). Test with 3–5 people through a web link or test build | **$0** | Does the recurring-trip, driver-detour flow feel better than Liftango? |
| **2. Pilot** (1–2 months) | ~10 real EA colleagues, cost-sharing only, written consent. Needs store accounts to hand out the app (or keep it a web app) | **~$0–150** + ~$0–25/month hosting | Do people keep using it after week 2? How many matches per week? |
| **3. Make it legal and safe** | BC lawyer (cost-sharing, ICBC, terms, privacy, points rules); platform insurance | **~$1.5K–5K + ~$1–3K/yr insurance** | Is there a funding source (employer fee, grant, sponsor)? |
| **4. Grow** | More employers, public launch, support | Scales with use (maps ≈ $25–100/month at ~100 riders) | Revenue or grants cover costs? |

## Maximum exposure by stage
- Stages 0–1: **$0**.
- Through stage 2: **under ~$450** for the first year even if hosting runs the full 12 months ($144 fixed + $0–300 hosting).
- Stage 3 is the first real decision. By then there is evidence from real use.

## Rules to protect the budget
1. **No paid developers or agency** until stage 3 shows funding.
2. **No payment processing.** Points and cash gas-share settled between people, not through the app.
3. **Set usage alerts and spending caps** on maps and hosting from day one (Mapbox has no hard cap by default, per search results).
4. **Stop at any check that fails.** The cost of finding out is small by design.

## One thing that costs nothing but matters
Check your EA employment agreement (IP ownership, outside business activity) **before stage 1**. Selling to EA later would also involve their procurement and security review.
